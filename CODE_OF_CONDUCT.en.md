# Code of Conduct

[Čeština](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · **English**

We want the community around the RUin project to be safe, respectful and open.

## Our Pledge

As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to create a harassment-free environment where everyone is welcome regardless of age, physical or mental differences, ethnicity, sex, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

We pledge to act professionally, politely and with respect.

## Expected Behavior

- Use factual and respectful language.
- Accept and give constructive feedback.
- Assume good intentions, and ask for clarification before you judge.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Insults, belittling, trolling, personal attacks.
- Sexist, racist, homophobic or otherwise discriminatory statements.
- Harassment, threats or intimidation.
- Publishing other people's personal information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider unprofessional or harmful.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clear standards of behavior and for appropriate and fair responses to violations of this code.

They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests and other contributions that don't comply with this code.

## Reporting a Problem

If you experience or witness a violation of this code, write to the project maintainers:

- open an issue labeled `conduct` (if it's safe and appropriate), or
- contact the maintainers through the security channel described in [SECURITY.en.md](SECURITY.en.md).

Every report will be reviewed confidentially and with appropriate care.

## Enforcement

Serious or repeated violations may lead to:

- a warning,
- a temporary restriction on interaction,
- locking the discussion,
- removal of the ability to contribute to the project.

## Attribution

This code is inspired by the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1, adapted to the needs of this project.
